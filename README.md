# Chronicle of a murder foretold — A Murder Mystery Game

A 2D, story-driven murder mystery game developed by a team of five during a 48-hour game jam, based on the theme **"Connections."**

## The Story

At 4:30 AM, Annie wakes up to find her husband dead in his office. He was killed at 1:30 AM, and she has only a limited window to uncover the truth.

As Annie investigates, she discovers clues that lead her closer to the killer. But after every three clues, she becomes dizzy and falls asleep, only to wake up an hour earlier.

The day repeats at **4:30 AM, 3:30 AM, 2:30 AM, and finally 1:30 AM**.

Before each reset, Annie must decide who she suspects. Every choice matters. If she identifies the killer in time, she may save her husband. If she fails, she risks becoming the killer's next victim.

## Development Status

**Currently in development — First Working Prototype**

This project is an early playable prototype, and development is ongoing. The current version establishes the core investigation mechanics, interactive objects, dialogue choices, basic sound effects and audio cues, and time-loop concept. More features, refinements, and content may be added as development continues.

## Gameplay Features

* **Clue-based investigation:** Explore the environment and uncover evidence.
* **Time-loop mechanic:** Relive the same night, waking up an hour earlier after each cycle, and uncover different events.
* **Player-driven choices:** Choose a suspect as you piece together the mystery.
* **Branching outcomes:** Your deductions determine whether Annie can save her husband or meet the same fate.
* **Interaction with objects:** When an object is close enough to interact with, an E prompt appears above it.
  
## Built With

* **Godot Engine** — game development
* **GDScript** — gameplay logic and interactions
* **Dialogue Manager** — dialogue and branching choices
